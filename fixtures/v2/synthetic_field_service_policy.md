## 适用范围 / Eligibility

本政策仅适用于客户现场服务工程师执行经批准的现场服务任务；普通员工的常规商务差旅应参考员工差旅政策。金额相近并不意味着两份政策可以互换。

This policy applies only to field service engineers on approved customer site assignments. General employees on regular business travel must use the Employee Travel policy. Similar amounts do not make these policies interchangeable.

## 国内现场服务住宿 / Domestic field service lodging

客户现场服务工程师在中国境内执行现场服务任务，住宿报销上限为每人每晚 550 元人民币。超过该金额须由现场服务负责人事先批准，而非普通员工所在部门主管批准。

For domestic customer site assignments in China, field service engineers may claim up to CNY 550 per person per night for lodging. Any excess requires advance approval by the field service lead, not the general employee's department manager.

## 海外现场服务住宿 / Overseas field service lodging

客户现场服务工程师赴中国境外执行现场服务任务，住宿报销上限为每人每晚 1550 元人民币等值金额。报销必须关联已批准的现场服务工单。

For overseas customer site assignments outside China, field service engineers may claim up to the equivalent of CNY 1550 per person per night for lodging. Claims must reference an approved field service work order.

## 审核与生效 / Review and effective date

费用由现场服务负责人审核。本合成样例自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起生效，不能作为真实报销依据。

Expenses are reviewed by the field service lead. This synthetic fixture takes effect on January 1, 2026 and must not be used for actual expense claims.
