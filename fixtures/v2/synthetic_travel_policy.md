## 适用范围 / Eligibility

本政策适用于普通员工的常规商务差旅。客户现场服务工程师执行现场服务任务时，应参考单独的现场服务差旅政策。

This policy covers regular business travel by general employees. Field service engineers performing customer site assignments must consult the separate Field Service Travel policy.

## 国内住宿 / Domestic lodging

普通员工在中国境内出差，住宿报销上限为每人每晚 500 元人民币。该金额仅含住宿，不包括餐饮。超出上限的部分须事先取得部门主管的书面批准。

For domestic business travel within China, general employees may claim up to CNY 500 per person per night for lodging. Meals are excluded. Amounts above the limit require written approval from the department manager before the expense.

## 海外住宿 / Overseas lodging

普通员工赴中国境外出差，住宿报销上限为每人每晚 1500 元人民币等值金额。汇率采用报销提交当天的公司财务汇率。该标准不适用于客户现场服务任务。

For overseas business travel outside China, general employees may claim up to the equivalent of CNY 1500 per person per night for lodging. Use the company's finance exchange rate on the submission date. This limit does not cover customer field service assignments.

## 审核与生效 / Review and effective date

费用由部门主管审核。本合成样例自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起生效。未明确规定的费用不能仅凭本样例推定可报销。

Expenses are reviewed by the department manager. This synthetic fixture takes effect on January 1, 2026. An expense absent from this fixture must not be assumed reimbursable.
